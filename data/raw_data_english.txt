476. V. PRODUCT STORAGE
477. STORAGE BY MATERIAL TYPE
478. 
479. Product storage details: 
480. 
481. * Knit fabric: After washing, products must be hung flat to avoid stretching.
482. 
483. * Silk, lace, chiffon fabric should be hand washed.
484. 
485. * Plain fabric, twill, khaki without combination or rhinestone decoration can be machine washed.
486. 
487. * Plain fabric, twill, khaki with color combination or lace, silk, rhinestone decoration should be hand washed.
488. 
489. * Jeans should avoid machine washing as it will cause fading. If washing, turn the product inside out when washing, close buttons, zip up, do not wash with light colored items, avoid color transfer to other products. 
490. 
491. STORAGE FOR PRODUCT SHAPE
492. 
493. 1. Should use alternately: 
494.     – An extremely important note to preserve your product is to use it alternately or in rotation. 
495.     – If used continuously without rest, they will quickly lose their shape. 
496.     – The time hanging on the hanger is when the suit regains its shape. 
497.     – Besides, if you don't have time to clean, don't throw them in a dirty laundry basket but hang them in a well-ventilated place to avoid moisture and     
498.       keep them always dry and clean.
499.  
500. 2. Do not put too many items in shirt/pants pockets when wearing: 
501.     – Putting many items in pockets (inside shirt pockets, outside pockets or pants pockets) not only ruins the lines of the outfit    
502.       but in the long run it also destroys the shape of the clothing, stretches the pocket edges. 
503.     – If you are wearing a well-fitted suit, it's best to only carry essentials like cash, phone and credit card.
504.  
505. 3. Clean quickly with a soft brush or lint roller to remove crumbs and dust from the suit before taking it for washing:
506.      – This is the best way to extend the life of natural fibers as well as the fabric material of the clothing.
507.  
508. 4. Absolutely do not wash with water at home or machine wash to ensure shape and product quality. 
509.  
510. 5.  Hang them up after use: 
511.     – Choose a hanger with thick, rounded shoulders when hanging shirts and suits (preferably a specialized hanger) to both maintain the product shape while creating an airy space. 
512.     – Always neatly fold products into clean, specialized garment bags when not in use.

514. 6. Iron products with steam: 
515.     – Dry ironing will be too hot and can burn fine soft fiber fabrics. 
516.     – It's best to use a steam iron to flatten the surface of the clothing, steam will help expand the fibers and prevent strange smells, and also helps remove small stains.

518. STORAGE FOR PRODUCT COLOR
519. 
520. * Products should be washed immediately without soaking to avoid color bleeding, color bleeding, distinguish color and fabric type to avoid fabric fading. Do not wash products with strong bleach soap, should wash with diluted soap.
521. 
522. * Products that can be machine washed should only be on gentle wash mode, medium spin level and products of the same color and fabric type should be separated when washing.
523. 
524. * Products should be dried in a ventilated place, avoid direct sunlight which can easily fade or bleach color, should dry clothes by hanging in windy spots to better maintain fabric color.
525. 
526. * 100% cotton fabrics should not be hung on clothes hooks but should be spread evenly on clotheslines to avoid fabric cracking.
527. 
528. * When ironing products with an iron and using steam mode, the product will be easier to iron, cool and not burn, maintain product color beautifully and durably. Iron temperature depends on each type of fabric.
